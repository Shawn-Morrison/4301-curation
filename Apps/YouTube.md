# YouTube

**Category:** Intermediate > Entertainment

YouTube is placed at the intermediate level because it offers a large range of entertainment and learning videos, but its search results, recommendations, advertisements, and comments can require more judgment. A future entry can add setup notes and a safety checklist after they are reviewed.

# Risk Level: 5

This application has a risk level of 5 because anyone can post videos, which means it can blur reality for the consumer. Users should be aware of how to determine fact from fiction and entertainment from news.
